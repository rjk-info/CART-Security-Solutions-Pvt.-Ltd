Key Responsibilities

Professional Responsibilities for Controlled & Secure Environments

Bouncer responsibilities can vary depending on the venue, event and assignment. The role is focused on maintaining a professional security presence, supporting access management, observing the environment and carrying out assigned duties with calm and responsible conduct.

01

Maintaining a Professional Security Presence

Maintain a calm, confident and disciplined security presence appropriate to the requirements of the assigned venue or environment.

02

Supporting Access Management

Support appropriate entry, exit and access-related responsibilities according to the requirements of the venue or assignment.

03

Observing Crowd & Venue Conditions

Remain attentive to activity within the assigned environment and support appropriate security awareness around entrances, gathering areas and managed spaces.

04

Supporting Orderly Environments

Assist in maintaining an organised and professional environment through appropriate communication and responsible security support.

05

Communication & Coordination

Support clear communication and professional coordination with relevant venue, event or security personnel as required by the assignment.
