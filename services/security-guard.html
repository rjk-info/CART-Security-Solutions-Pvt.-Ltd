Professional Duties. Responsible Security Support.

Security Guard responsibilities can vary depending on the premises and assignment. The role is focused on maintaining a professional presence, supporting site security requirements and carrying out assigned duties with discipline and responsibility.

01

Maintaining a Professional Security Presence

Maintain an alert, disciplined and professional presence according to the security requirements of the assigned premises.

02

Supporting Access Security

Support entry, exit and access-related security responsibilities according to the procedures and requirements of the premises.

03

Observing the Assigned Area

Remain attentive to the surrounding environment and support appropriate awareness of activity within the assigned area.

04

Following Site Responsibilities

Carry out assigned security duties and site-related responsibilities in a professional and responsible manner.

05

Communication & Coordination

Support clear communication and appropriate coordination with relevant personnel as required by the security assignment.
